Section 3 of the Development Agreement states: "Notwithstanding any restrictions, rights or privileges under applicable laws or the City's Development Code and other ordinances, the Permitted Uses shall be allowed on the Property and the Prohibited Uses are expressly prohibited on the Property."

The agreement also provides (§1.04.A): "In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and the SMDC or the City's Codes and Ordinances, this Agreement shall control." The initial term runs 45 years from January 2023, with options to extend through 2083.

Coverage note: The development agreement covers the approximately 2,017 acres held by Franklin Mountain San Marcos I, LP. A separate ~75-acre parcel (CAD ID 28052, acquired April 2024) is held by a distinct entity — Franklin Mountain San Marcos 75, LP — and is not a party to this development agreement. The permitted and prohibited uses on this page do not apply to that parcel.

Note — Data Centers & the June 16, 2026 City Ordinance
On June 16, 2026, the City of San Marcos passed an ordinance restricting data centers within city limits. Because data centers are an explicitly named Permitted Use in Exhibit D (item 45) — and because the development agreement constitutes a vested-rights permit under Texas Local Government Code Chapter 245 and controls over subsequent city ordinances — the owner would have a strong legal argument that traditional data centers remain permitted on this property notwithstanding the new ordinance.

However, the SMDC's updated data center definition (§5.1.7.10, effective 2025) explicitly includes cryptocurrency mining, while the development agreement's custom definition does not. The status of crypto mining facilities on this site is legally ambiguous.
