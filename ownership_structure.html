Structural Notes

  • Why "Franklin Mountain"? Paul Foster named his entire investment empire after the Franklin Mountains in El Paso, TX — the same brand is used across energy, real estate, aviation, sports, and packaging ventures.
  • Why two separate LPs (I and Fund)? "FM San Marcos I, LP" (Aug 2021) is the primary land-holding vehicle. "FM San Marcos Fund, LP" (Oct 2021, formed 6 weeks later) is the investor/co-investor vehicle — a common Texas structure where the Fund LP holds LP interests in the operating "I" LP.
  • Why a separate "75" LP? The ~75-acre Pettus Survey parcel (Caldwell CAD ID 28052) was acquired in Apr 2024 by a distinct entity with its own CAD Owner ID (242650 vs. 234440). Likely a parcel-specific SPE — possibly structured for a specific tenant lease or separate financing tranche.
  • Scarborough San Marcos GP LLC is the formal GP entity in all three LPs. It does not appear in searchable web corporate databases — only in Caldwell County deed records and Caldwell CAD property records. It is the vehicle through which Scarborough Lane exercises operational control.
  • Landmark Interests vs. Scarborough Lane: They share the same address and phone number (16380 Addison Rd; 972-380-5900); Landmark's website redirects to scarboroughlanedevelopment.com. Landmark handles the brokerage, land acquisition, and property tax side; Scarborough Lane handles development and GP management.
  • AgTrust, ACA (formerly Lone Star, ACA) holds ~$52.8M in financing secured against the site. This represents the developer's debt posture and is relevant to understanding any future land sales or lease financing.
  • Partners Real Estate (Houston) is named as "co-developer" in public press releases but plays the marketing/leasing role, not a capital or ownership role.
