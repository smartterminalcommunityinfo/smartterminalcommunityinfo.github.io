Structural Notes

  • Why "Franklin Mountain"? Paul Foster named his entire investment empire after the Franklin Mountains in El Paso, TX - the same brand is used across energy, real estate, aviation, sports, and packaging ventures.
  • Why two separate LPs (I and Fund)? "FM San Marcos I, LP" (Aug 2021) is the primary land-holding vehicle. "FM San Marcos Fund, LP" (Oct 2021, formed 6 weeks later) is the investor/co-investor vehicle - a common Texas structure where the Fund LP holds LP interests in the operating "I" LP.
  • Why a separate "75" LP? The ~75-acre Pettus Survey parcel (Caldwell CAD ID 28052) was acquired in Apr 2024 by a distinct entity with its own CAD Owner ID (242650 vs. 234440). Likely a parcel-specific SPE - possibly structured for a specific tenant lease or separate financing tranche.
  • Scarborough San Marcos GP LLC is the formal GP entity in all three LPs. It does not appear in searchable web corporate databases - only in Caldwell County deed records and Caldwell CAD property records. It is the vehicle through which Scarborough Lane exercises operational control.
  • Landmark Interests vs. Scarborough Lane: They share the same address and phone number (16380 Addison Rd; 972-380-5900); Landmark's website redirects to scarboroughlanedevelopment.com. Landmark handles the brokerage, land acquisition, and property tax side; Scarborough Lane handles development and GP management.
  • AgTrust, ACA (formerly Lone Star, ACA) holds ~$52.8M in financing secured against the site. This represents the developer's debt posture and is relevant to understanding any future land sales or lease financing.
  • Partners Real Estate (Houston) is named as "co-developer" in public press releases but plays the marketing/leasing role, not a capital or ownership role.
  • Franklin Mountain Communities, LLC is a renamed Scarborough entity. Per OpenCorporates/TX SOS (#0803184088), it traded as "Scarborough ELP Development LLC" from Dec 10, 2018 until Jul 29, 2020, when it was renamed to "Franklin Mountain Communities LLC." Its registered address is 123 W Mills Ave Ste 600, El Paso - and it shares both its registered agent (Capitol Corporate Services, Austin) and its listed officers (Sandra Mendez, Scott Weaver, William Kell) with Franklin Mountain Investments, LLC. This is a same-entity rename plus shared officers, not merely a shared address, making it the most direct documented link between the Foster "Franklin Mountain" brand and the Scarborough Lane group. (A Texas Association of Builders directory listing for this entity at 16380 Addison Rd does not match the primary SOS record and appears to be stale.)

Jurisdiction & Development Agreement Coverage

The ~2,058-acre assemblage spans 9 Caldwell CAD parcels across two ownership entities. Historically, all of it sat within the City of San Marcos's Extraterritorial Jurisdiction (ETJ) - the original 2019 agreement is literally named the "Chapter 380 Economic Development Incentive and ETJ Development Agreement." Only a fraction has actually been annexed into the city limits: Ordinance 2019-01 annexed ~733.6 acres (as later corrected by a Nov 2024 technical amendment; originally 734.6 acres) and Ordinance 2024-27 annexed a separate 7.553-acre road strip for Energy Parkway, for a combined ~741 acres in city limits. A companion ordinance passed the same night as Ord. 2019-01, Ordinance 2019-02, rezoned that same ~734.6-acre footprint to Heavy Industrial - both ordinances were originally proposed at first reading for a larger ~934.34 acres, then both reduced to the identical smaller footprint at second/final reading. A much larger annexation attempt in 2023 (Ord. 2023-32, ~619.755 acres, paired with a rezoning of ~588.821 acres to Heavy Industrial) was formally withdrawn by the developer in June 2023 and never took effect. The remaining acreage - roughly 1,300+ of the ~2,058 total - stays in unincorporated Caldwell County, outside City zoning jurisdiction entirely. The site's records don't map the annexed ~741 acres onto specific CAD parcel boundaries, so it isn't possible to say from this site's sources exactly which of the 9 parcels are fully inside city limits versus only partially so.

Coverage under the governing Development Agreement (originally 2019, amended 2023) tracks ownership, not jurisdiction: it applies to all 8 parcels held by Franklin Mountain San Marcos I, LP, regardless of whether those parcels sit in the city or the county. It explicitly excludes the ~72.834-acre parcel (CAD ID 28052) held separately by Franklin Mountain San Marcos 75, LP - see the Permitted & Prohibited Uses page for what that means for that parcel. That parcel's historical ETJ status isn't independently confirmed by a source on this site, unlike the other 8 parcels, which are directly tied to the 2019 ETJ Development Agreement.
