Two-phase ERCOT role: Foster donated to Abbott, Patrick, and Phelan - the three officials whose designees formed the ERCOT Board Selection Committee. That committee chose Foster as the inaugural ERCOT Board Chair (Oct 2021). He chaired the board for ~3 years, overseeing transmission planning and large-load policy. ERCOT is exempt from Texas open meeting laws.

2022
2022 - Large Flexible Load Task Force ERCOT (Foster as Chair) establishes LFLTF - creates framework for industrial customers ≥75 MW to interconnect. Directly relevant to data center or heavy manufacturing tenants at Axis.

2022 - LCRA Hays-Kendall corridor begun $400M, 60-mile transmission rebuild from Hays Energy Substation (San Marcos) through Comal/Kendall counties. Under ERCOT 2022 Regional Transmission Plan approved during Foster's chairmanship.
Mar–Jul 2022 FM completes land assembly: Van Lee LLC (0.95 ac), Tack Redwood/Neumann (130.3 ac), Best David Matthew (143.4 ac).

Jun 2022 Developer withdraws first annexation attempt (AN-22-20 / ZC-23-01).

2023 Regional Transmission Plan 173 projects (up from 89 in 2022). Central Texas / Austin corridor constraints identified. Approved under Foster's chairmanship. LCRA/Bluebonnet corridor serving Axis site part of this planning.
Jan 17, 2023 First Amended & Restated SMART Terminal Development Agreement adopted by San Marcos City Council (Caldwell Co. 2023-001629). FM's ~2,017 ac placed under new DA.

May 2023 Clayton Kendall writes San Marcos City Council re: project vision.

Jun 2024 - ERCOT role changes Foster steps down from the ERCOT Board (and as Chair). Abbott then directly appoints Foster to the ERCOT Board Selection Committee - the 3-person body that selects ERCOT board members. Foster transitions from being chosen by the committee to sitting on it.
Foster's tenure ends Jun 2024 Large-load framework architecture (NPRR1234/PGRR115) moves toward formal codification - will be adopted Apr 2025.

End 2025 (target) 69→138 kV upgrade at Axis site completing; two 345 kV lines already on property.

TPWD ERT advisory authority (§12.0011) - TPWD can formally advise every permitting agency on projects affecting fish & wildlife TPWD 2021–present Commissioner → Chair (Jun 2025). Sets ERT staffing, priorities, and review posture. Under §12.0011, TPWD recommendations reach TCEQ (WPP2 permit 2025-55457), Army Corps, TxDOT, and City of San Marcos permits. Active unresolved WPP2 violations include impervious cover >40%, 5 unprotected sensitive geological features, and post-construction peak flow increases. Structural WPP2 violations unresolved. TPWD ERT comment status unknown - request from TCEQ permit file.
Edwards Aquifer Habitat Conservation Plan (EAHCP) - TPWD is a mandatory statutory participant TPWD 2021–present Commissioner → Chair. TPWD's EAHCP participation is set at Commission level. EAHCP governs springflow protections for the Comal and San Marcos rivers - the receiving waters for the Edwards Aquifer recharge zone where Axis sits. Seven federally listed endangered species depend on San Marcos Springs springflow. TPWD's EAHCP posture shapes what flow conditions downstream development must protect.
