Maps & Site Exhibits

Engineering exhibits, survey plats, drainage/floodplain studies, and aerial maps for the TriTexas Logistics Park (formerly SMART Terminal / Axis Logistics Park), compiled from public information requests filed with the City of San Marcos and Caldwell County. Click any image to view full resolution.

All exhibits below are sourced from public records obtained via open records requests. Most are engineering drawings prepared by Kimley-Horn and Associates for Franklin Mountain San Marcos I, LP, marked "For Review Only" or "Not For Construction" unless otherwise noted - they represent proposed/planning-stage designs, not necessarily final as-built conditions.
