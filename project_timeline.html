City Council approves the original Cotton Center Consent Agreement and processes the Municipal Utility District (MUD) petition for the Cotton Center area in Caldwell County. February 4, 2020
Milestone City Council Martindale ETJ Boundary Agreement Adopted (Res. 2020-17R(b))
City Council brings back Resolution 2020-17R (renumbered 2020-17R(b) for this version) after the January 21 postponement and unanimously approves the ETJ boundary agreement with the City of Martindale, authorizing the Mayor or City Manager to execute it. Martindale's Council had already approved its side on January 21.
  • Resolves longstanding 1983 and 2007 ETJ boundary disagreements between the two cities; each city agrees not to challenge the validity of the other's earlier resolution (San Marcos re: the 1983 resolution; Martindale re: R132)
  • Martindale releases 161 acres of the SMART Terminal site from its claimed ETJ, allowing San Marcos to annex the full site
  • In exchange, San Marcos agrees to pay Martindale 30% of the annual property tax revenue generated by that 161-acre tract, from 2021 through 2051

Note: This resolves the specific inter-city ETJ boundary-line dispute. It does not reflect Martindale's stance on the SMART Terminal project itself - Martindale's City Council separately and formally opposed the project's heavy industrial zoning in April 2023, on flooding grounds.

Source: San Marcos Legistar - Res. 2020-17R(b); "Council approves Martindale ETJ agreement," San Marcos Record, Feb. 7, 2020; "San Marcos, Martindale ETJ Matters Come To Resolution," Corridor News, Feb. 2020

May 11, 2023
Milestone Project Publicly Renamed - "SMART Terminal" Becomes "AXIS Logistics Park"
Co-developers Scarborough Lane Development and Partners Real Estate issue a joint press release announcing the project - previously called SMART Terminal - is now AXIS Logistics Park, a master-planned industrial park spanning approximately 2,000 acres, with roughly 735 acres then zoned for industrial use. The release touts direct access to Loop 110 and proximity to IH-35, I-10, and SH-130, plus adjacency to Union Pacific Railway, BNSF Railway, and San Marcos Regional Airport. Notably, this announcement comes just one day after the City's own executive sessions (May 10, above) were still referring to the project internally as the "SMART Terminal Development Agreement" - City usage lagged the public rebrand by months.
  • Clayton Kendall (Scarborough Lane EVP, Industrial Division): "AXIS Logistics Park is open for business and will be the first industrial park of its scale in Central Texas"
  • John Colglazier (Partners Real Estate): calls the site "the obvious choice for logistics and shipping by national and international manufacturers"
