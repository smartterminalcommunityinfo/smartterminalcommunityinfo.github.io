Interactive Map

The TriTexas Logistics Park site (formerly SMART Terminal / Axis Logistics Park) and its surroundings, drawn from public records. Turn layers on and off in the panel, and click any shape for its source.

Background

Layers

More layers are being added from the developer's own exhibits: the container storage area, the project phases, the original Katerra tract, Energy Parkway and others. Developer-drawn layers will be marked as approximate.

How the layers were made

  • Parcels (TriTexas ownership, EDC Austin, Proto-Land) are the Caldwell Central Appraisal District's parcel outlines. Appraisal maps are close but are not surveys.
  • Caldwell 142 Reinvestment Zone #2 is traced from the 23 recorded boundary calls in Exhibit A to Caldwell County Order 01-2027 (Kimley-Horn survey dated October 27, 2026). The traced boundary closes within 0.02 feet and measures 611.01 acres, and it was placed on the map by fitting it to the appraisal district's outline of the 660.503-acre tract (average difference about 5 feet).
  • City limits and ETJ are from the City of San Marcos GIS. Floodplain is FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer (1% and 0.2% annual chance zones). Pipelines are from the Railroad Commission of Texas public pipeline map; the RRC notes its pipeline locations are approximate. Rail is from OpenStreetMap.
